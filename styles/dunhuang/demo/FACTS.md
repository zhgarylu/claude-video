# Facts used by the demo

The demo is a fiction about light and age. It puts no cave number, date, name or statistic on screen. The only real-world claim that shapes the picture is below; the voice-over and plaques say nothing that depends on it.

| Claim | Where it shows | Source | Quoted wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| Many figures in the Dunhuang murals have darkened because lead-based pigments oxidised through exposure to air and light. | The weathered state turns white lead and red lead areas grey-brown and dark (oxidation map). | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mogao_Caves (section on pigments), checked 2026-10-04 | "Many of the figures have darkened due to oxidation of the lead-based pigments from exposure to air and light." |

Open point: before any final cut states a fact on screen, replace this secondary source with the Dunhuang Academy or Getty Conservation Institute Mogao project pages (https://www.getty.edu/conservation/our_projects/field_projects/mogao/ was reachable but its text was not checked for this wording).
