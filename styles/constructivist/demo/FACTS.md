# FACTS: real-world claims shown in "Thirty Metres"

Checked on 2026-10-04 by reading the official World Athletics documents (not from memory). Only the claims below appear on screen.

Source A: World Athletics, *Technical Rules*, Book C2.1, official download: https://worldathletics.org/download/download?filename=febae412-b673-4523-8321-e1ed092421dc.pdf&urlslug=C2.1+-+Technical+Rules (cover: "In force from 1 November 2019").
Source B: World Athletics, *Competition and Technical Rule Amendments approved by Council, March 2025*: https://worldathletics.org/download/download?filename=83d2ccc8-b293-4354-9874-45e384adcff4.pdf&urlslug=Competition+and+Technical+Rule+Amendments+March+2025

| Shown on screen | Rule | Wording in the source |
|---|---|---|
| "4×100", "100 + 100 + 100 + 100", "FOUR RUNNERS ONE BATON" | A, TR 24.1 | "The standard distances shall be: 4 × 100m, 4 × 200m, …" |
| "30" (the zone is 30 m); "20 M BEFORE THE LINE", "10 M AFTER" (the zone's two parts either side of the scratch line) | A, TR 24.3; B, TR 24.3 amendment | A: "In the 4 x 100m and the 4 x 200m relays … each takeover zone shall be 30m long, of which the scratch line is 20m from the start of the zone." B (March 2025) amends the rule only to add the 4 × 100m Mixed and keeps: "each takeover zone shall be 30m long, of which the scratch line is 20m from the beginning of the zone." The 10 m after the line is derived (30 − 20). |
| "PASS IT IN THE BOX", the pass beyond the edge of the zone, "OUTSIDE THE BOX", "DISQUALIFIED" | A, TR 24.7 | "The baton shall be passed within the takeover zone. … Passing of the baton outside the takeover zone shall result in disqualification." |
| "THREE EXCHANGES A RACE" | derived: four runners, so three hand-overs (not a quotation) | n/a |

Not used: the 20 m "changeover box" figure from the World Athletics discipline page (https://worldathletics.org/disciplines/relays/4x100-metres-relay), which differs from the rulebook; the film follows the rulebook. "Box" in the film's lettering means the takeover zone.

The runners, lane, colours and team are fictional.
